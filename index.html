Our Story

How it began

We met in the University — Saumi was two years younger than me, and I was her senior. Instantly, we were attracted to each other; we knew the other was someone special.

From the moment we first met, sparks started to fly. We ate Pani poori and, despite Saumi having a terrible cold, she enjoyed Orange juice, saying it’s vitamin C, it’s good for us.

That night I dropped her off. She glanced a longing look at me, and left — taking my heart permanently.

Then we started to have long glances at each other when passing in the corridor — smiles, blushes, and endless chats in the evening.

And then, on the most beautiful day for a lover — 14th of February — I asked her out. Holding her hand, looking at her eyes, I nervously told her… asked… shall we embark on a journey together?

And… She said Yes!

That was 06 years ago! 06 years, filled with laughter, cuddles, emotions, and passion… A time filled with nothing but love, care, respect and admiration for one another!

Today, after a long journey, we have come to the mountaintop — only to embark on another adventure.

Poem

A few lines, in quiet grace.

Where the quiet waters meet the shore,

From "you" and "me" to the strength of "we,"

Rooted in values, in honor and grace,
We find our home in each other's embrace.
With quiet certainty and hearts so true,

Beneath the sky, where the lake breeze blows,

Hand in hand, as the journey begins,
