Our Story

How it began

We met at university — Saumi was two years younger than me, and I was her senior. From the very first day, something felt different… like we’d found someone special.

Our first little moments still make us smile — pani poori, evening chats, shy glances in the corridor, and that quiet feeling of “this might be it.”

And then… she said yes.

Years have passed since then — years filled with laughter, warmth, and a love that grew steadier with time. Today, we’re stepping into our next chapter with full hearts, grateful for everyone who shaped us into who we are.
