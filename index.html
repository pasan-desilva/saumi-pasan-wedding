Our Story

How it began

We met at university — Saumi was two years younger than me, and I was her senior. From the very first moments, we felt that quiet certainty that the other was someone special.

One evening, we shared pani poori — and even with a terrible cold, Saumi insisted on orange juice with a smile, saying, “It’s vitamin C… it’s good for us.” That night, when I dropped her off, she looked back with a longing glance — and I’m not sure I ever got my heart back after that.

After that came corridor glances, shy smiles, blushing, and long evening chats — and on the most beautiful day for lovers, the 14th of February, I held her hand, looked into her eyes, and asked if we could begin a journey together.

And… she said yes!

Years have passed since then — years filled with laughter, comfort, care, and a love that kept growing quietly, day by day. And today, we take the next step, hand in hand, with hearts full of gratitude.
