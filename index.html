Our Story

How it began

We met at the university. Saumi was two years younger, and I was her senior. From the very first moment, there was something quietly unmistakable between us. Nothing was spoken aloud, but somehow, we both knew the other was someone special.

That first evening stays with us fondly. We shared pani poori, laughed easily, and despite having a terrible cold, Saumi insisted on having orange juice, smiling and saying it was vitamin C and that it would be good for us. Later that night, as I dropped her off, she turned back and looked at me just for a moment. In that moment, without either of us realizing it, something changed. She walked away carrying my heart with her.

After that, it was always in the little things. Long glances when we passed each other in the corridors. Quiet smiles. Gentle blushes. Evenings filled with conversations that seemed to stretch endlessly, where time slipped by without notice.

And… she said yes.

The 14th of February remains one of our most treasured days. Nervous and hopeful, I held her hand, looked into her eyes, and asked if we could embark on this journey together. She smiled, paused for just a heartbeat, and said yes.

Years have passed since then. Years filled with laughter and comfort, growth and understanding, tenderness and passion. Years of choosing each other every single day with care, respect, and deep admiration. Today, after a journey that has shaped us quietly and deeply, we stand together ready to begin a new chapter. One rooted in love, values, and a shared sense of home.
